01The question

The test is not whether an attack works.

The harder question is what happens after an agent encounters information it was never authorized to follow. Does it complete the legitimate task, preserve the boundary, and leave a trace that makes the decision inspectable?

02The method

Make the pressure visible.

03The evidence

Separate the outcome from the story.

A refusal can look safe while hiding a failed task. A completed task can look useful while concealing an unauthorized effect. The instrument keeps those outcomes apart.
