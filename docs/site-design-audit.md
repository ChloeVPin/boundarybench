# BoundaryBench site design audit

This audit records the visual references and translation decisions behind the current public research studio.

## Reference pass

Reviewed September 3, 2026:

* [OpenAI home](https://openai.com/)
* [OpenAI research overview](https://openai.com/research/)
* [OpenAI safety overview](https://openai.com/safety/)
* [GPT 5.5 announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-5/)

The references were used as design research only. BoundaryBench keeps its own name, mark, research language, palette, and repository links.

## Section by section

### Global navigation

The reference pages use a short header with a clear wordmark, a small set of high value destinations, quiet secondary links, and one visually distinct action. The navigation stays legible because it does not compete with the page premise.

BoundaryBench uses the same information hierarchy for Research, Protocol, Evidence, and Public record. GitHub remains visible as the canonical source, while Read the brief acts as the primary entry action. The circular mark is original to BoundaryBench and uses a chartreuse center to signal the authority theme.

### First viewport

The reference pages establish one idea before introducing detail. Their first screens use a centered sans serif headline, a short supporting paragraph, restrained controls, and a large visual or content block with generous breathing room.

BoundaryBench now opens with Researching the edge of authority. The question is stated in plain language, the two actions are compact pills, and the original boundary field artwork spans the content width below the premise. The image is treated as a research surface rather than a decorative background, with a small study label and controlled condition metadata.

### Focus and concept cards

The research and safety pages use modular cards to introduce a small number of concepts. Each card has an unmistakable title, a short explanation, a contained visual treatment, and a path to deeper material. Borders and tonal changes provide structure without heavy ornament.

BoundaryBench translates that pattern into three focus cards for Provenance, Position, and Repetition. Each card has a single signal color, a compact visual vocabulary, and a direct link into the repository. The cards explain why each factor matters before a reader reaches the full protocol.

### Long form research content

The announcement page demonstrates how a minimal visual system can support dense material. A narrow reading column, large line height, thin rules, and small metadata labels make long form evidence feel calm and inspectable.

BoundaryBench separates its statement, metric, protocol, and evidence sections into distinct surfaces. Large headings identify the question, a four column metric band gives the study scale a visual anchor, and the protocol table exposes the design without requiring a reader to open a notebook or terminal.

### Color and typography

Across the reference pages, black and white establish the base, while muted grays control hierarchy. Color appears in focused moments such as media, calls to action, or emphasis. The typography is primarily a single modern sans serif system with tight display tracking and comfortable body text. This keeps the research voice contemporary without relying on decorative type.

BoundaryBench uses one system sans stack throughout the site. Its own palette assigns clear roles:

* Black and paper establish the primary reading surfaces.
* Chartreuse marks authority, action, and the closing invitation.
* Cyan marks measured conditions and protocol levels.
* Cobalt marks research navigation and structural labels.
* Pale blue carries the evidence section and gives the analytical surface a distinct change of temperature.

### Footer and onward path

The references finish with a quiet footer and a clear next step. The page does not trap the reader in a campaign surface. It returns attention to the product, the research, or the source material.

BoundaryBench closes with a direct invitation to read, run, and question the instrument. The footer points to GitHub, the research record, the brand charter, and responsible disclosure. The repository remains the canonical record and the site remains the entry point.

## Design principles carried forward

1. State the research question before presenting the system.
2. Use spacing and alignment as the primary visual language.
3. Keep every section attached to a concrete research artifact.
4. Use color to encode meaning instead of decoration.
5. Keep the source of truth one click away.
6. Make the site feel authored while preserving independent research identity.
